# Comprehensive Analytics & Growth KPI Framework

## 1. Product Engagement & UI Health Metrics

### A. Form Completion Rate (FCR)
- **Mathematical Definition:**
  $$\text{FCR} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Unique Audits Triggered}}{\text{Total Unique Landing Sessions}} \right) \times 100$$
- **Target Threshold:** Baseline target greater than 45%.
- **Tracking Purpose:** Measures the layout's psychological friction. If this drops below the target threshold, it indicates that the number of inputs in the tool lists is overwhelming users before they click the trigger button.

### B. Footprint Selection Intensity (FSI)
- **Definition:** The mean count of active software traces checked by a user during an evaluation loop.
- **Segment Targets:**
  - Solo Developers: 2 to 3 active selections.
  - SMB / Agencies: 4 or more selections.
  - Enterprise: 6 or more selections.
- **Tracking Purpose:** Identifies whether the tool list reflects what tech companies are actually spending money on in the wild.

### C. Interface Bounce Rate (IBR)
- **Definition:** The percentage of users who load the application index page but exit without interacting with a single metadata selector or checkbox.
- **Target Threshold:** Kept strictly below 28% through our distraction-free, zero-onboarding layout strategy.

## 2. Revenue Funnel & Lead Capture KPIs

### A. Lead Generation Conversion Rate (LGCR)
- **Mathematical Definition:**
  $$\text{LGCR} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Corporate Email Form Submissions}}{\text{Total Rendered Audit Report Layout Views}} \right) \times 100$$
- **Target Threshold:** Balanced at a strict target of 12% across organic traffic segments.
- **Tracking Purpose:** Validates the persuasiveness of the Bento Grid dashboard report. If the savings card displays a high waste value, the psychological incentive to lock parameters should drive high submission metrics.

### B. High-Value Consultation Qualification Rate (CQR)
- **Definition:** The proportion of captured lead inputs that programmatically trigger the `requiresCredexConsultation` flag.
- **Algorithmic Trigger Filter:** Total annual software waste exceeds $2,400 with a team size larger than 5 seats.
- **Target Tracking Goal:** 4% of total lead generation volume, representing high-intent pipeline leads optimized for enterprise enterprise contract placement calls.

### C. System Retention & Parametric Adjustments Index
- **Definition:** Measures how many times a user clicks "Adjust Input Parameters & Recalculate" to stress-test different hypothetical budgeting setups.
- **Goal KPI:** A healthy average of 2.4 adjustments per session, confirming that users treat the dashboard as an interactive financial planning sandbox.
